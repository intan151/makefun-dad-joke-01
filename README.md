One dad joke, at most 200 characters — a pun that lands cleanly, a groan
you can hear across the dinner table, delivered deadpan. Entry for the
[Dad Joke Showdown](https://make.fun/c/u-mfnakmal-dad-joke-showdown-1d2ce3e5)
competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

## The joke

> I told my son I was going to open a bakery for pigeons. He said, "That's a
> crumby idea." I said, "Exactly. That's the point."

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only, no network, no randomness) writes
the single joke to `/output/entry.txt` (125 characters). Run it twice and the
output is byte-identical.
